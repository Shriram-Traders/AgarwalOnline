# Every UI change ships for desktop and phone

Nineteen of twenty shoppers order from a phone, so a shop screen is designed and measured at phone width first. Anything new — a page, a section, a component, a form — still ships for both widths in the same change, not "phone now, desktop later".

- The shop's own screens are styled phone-first in `src/app/shop.css` (desktop added at 761px): the yellow header band, section tabs, the shelf rail, the welcome card, shelf tiles and the compact product card. Older phone rules live in the `@media (max-width: 760px)` blocks of `src/app/globals.css` (tablet steps at 1023/900px). Reuse the phone patterns already there before inventing one: bottom tab bar (Home, Categories, Family, Basket, You), the two-row header (name and delivery promise scroll away; the search stays pinned), a rail of shelves beside two columns of products, swipe rails (`.home .product-grid`), the sticky basket bar with its free-delivery line, the pinned buy bar on single-pack product pages, the bottom sheet (`ShareSheet`), `DataTable` rows that become cards at phone width (760px and below; a phone turned sideways keeps the table).
- The category page (`/catalog`) runs in the browser once it has the whole catalogue: `CatalogBrowser` (`src/components/catalog-browser.tsx`) opens on the server's list, loads `/api/catalog/all` once (about 20 KB compressed, CDN-cached 30 s), then switches shelves, searches, sorts and filters on the phone with `history.pushState`, and shows more products as you scroll (48 at a time; photos load lazily). Browsing itself lives in `src/lib/catalog/browse.ts`, which the server's `catalog()` runs too: change search or sorting there, never in one place only; `tests/catalog.integration.test.ts` checks the phone and the server give the same list. A card the browser draws is `ProductCardView` (`product-card-view.tsx`); `ProductCard` is its server wrapper.
- `.product-card`, `.price-row`, `.hero-card`, `.deal-card`, `.header`, `.top-strip` and `.catalog-chips` are shared with the school pages, shared lists, the wishlist and staff pages. Change them in place and look at those pages too; search for other users before deleting a rule.
- Colours come from the theme the owners pick in Settings → Shop front and colours (`src/lib/settings/themes.ts`, written onto `<html>` by the root layout). Style with the tokens (`--brand`, `--offer`, `--band` / `--on-band`, `--page`, `--tint-1` to `--tint-5`), never a literal colour, and look at a dark-header theme too. A new theme goes in that file; `tests/themes.test.ts` refuses one whose words would be hard to read.
- Tap targets are at least 24px tall (44px for primary controls); no page may scroll sideways; fixed bars must not cover content (pad for `--bottom-nav-h` and `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`).
- `tests/e2e/layout.spec.ts` finds every `page.tsx` under `src/app` and checks it at desktop (chromium) and phone (Pixel 7) width for sideways scroll, content running off the edge and small tap targets. A new dynamic route fails until it gets a sample there. Run it after any UI change: `npx playwright test tests/e2e/layout.spec.ts` (needs the local test MongoDB on 27028). `SHOT_DIR=.local/shots` also saves a screenshot of every page at both widths — look at them. Before a change that touches layout broadly, also run `SCREENS=android npx playwright test tests/e2e/layout.spec.ts`: every page again on 8 more Android screens (320 wide, the common 360 × 780, 480 wide, a 640 tablet, an opened foldable, phones and a tablet sideways), about an hour. A view of a page that lays out differently (a picked shelf) goes in `VIEWS` in the spec, and `ONLY=/catalog,/catalog/shelf` checks just those routes, in about 90 seconds.

# Staff pages follow the same admin patterns

- A list of records is a `DataTable` with a `FilterBar` (search plus the one or two filters people need) and a count line; the first column is the record's name, linking to its own page or an `?edit=` panel above the table. Never render a stack of open edit forms or accordions, one per record. On a phone each record is one short row (its name, then each value with its column name) and the filters other than the search box (`name="q"`) wait behind a Filters button, except one marked `className="filter-keep"` (Products' Category); both live in `src/app/staff.css`. Every `FilterBar` answers as you type or pick (`LiveFilter`, `src/components/filter-live.tsx`, which hides its button); put `LiveFilter` in any other GET search form a staff page has.
- Page chrome is `PageHeading` (eyebrow "Run the store" or "Owner", a one-sentence lead, the primary action in `aside`) and a `breadcrumb` on detail pages.
- A new staff page goes into the menu (`STAFF_NAV` in `src/lib/staff/nav.ts`) and the workspace search (`src/lib/staff/search-index.ts`); a new setting gets an `id` anchor and a search entry pointing at it. The workspace search also finds real products, orders and customers as you type (`src/lib/staff/records.ts` via `/api/staff/search`, each kind behind its own permission, with chips to narrow it); a new kind of record people look up goes there.
- Every setting lives on one Settings page (`/super-admin`), shown one section at a time: a row in its list (with what it's set to now and any warning) opens a `data-settings-section` by its `#id`. A new setting goes into an existing section; a new section also gets a row in that list. No second settings page.
- Money is typed in rupees with `MoneyInput` (named `…Rupees`, converted by `formWithPaise`); number-like fields use `inputMode` + `pattern`, not `type="number"`.
- Validation errors go through `plainMessage` in `src/lib/form-errors.ts`, so they name the field in plain English.
- `ActionForm confirmMessage` only for money, publishing or hard-to-undo actions; the dialog reads "<submit>?" with a "Yes, …" button.
- Times use `<When at>` (relative, exact IST on hover); audit codes are shown through `auditLabel` with the raw code beside them.
